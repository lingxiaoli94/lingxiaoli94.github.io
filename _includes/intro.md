I'm a Member of Technical Staff at [Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/) working on post-training. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at [Netflix](https://research.netflix.com/), where I worked at the intersection of AI and games. I explored AI-powered game experiences: the new, subversive forms of interaction that become possible when AI is not merely used to build a game, but runs within it—responding, adapting, and creating alongside the player. I also studied the unique AI research problems that games pose, such as teaching AI to understand and emulate human players, and ultimately to model what makes a game fun.
Before that, I received a PhD at MIT advised by [Justin Solomon](https://people.csail.mit.edu/jsolomon/), a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics and an M.S. in Mathematics, both at Stanford University.
